About the Studio

Every piece in Verdant & Vellum is finished by hand. Backgrounds are removed manually along the alpha channel so edges stay clean on any paper colour, then each image is upscaled to a full 300 DPI. The result is artwork that drops straight into your journal spread, sticker sheet or product mockup without a halo or a jagged edge. You buy it, you download it, you start creating.
